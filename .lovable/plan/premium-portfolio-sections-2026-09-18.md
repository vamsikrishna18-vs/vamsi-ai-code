# Premium Portfolio Sections

## Goal
Bring the About, Education, Skills, Projects, Certifications, Learning, Contact, and footer sections up to the same visual standard as the refreshed hero while preserving all supplied content and honest placeholders.

## Planned changes
- Add stronger section introductions, subtle background treatments, numbered accents, and consistent spacing between sections.
- Rework About into a premium profile composition with a refined VK portrait placeholder and clearer journey/interest highlights.
- Upgrade Education into a more visual timeline with a prominent current-study entry and polished completed-study entries.
- Give Skills distinct category treatments, interactive card accents, and more scannable skill tags without proficiency scores.
- Make Featured Projects the strongest post-hero section with a large editorial placeholder and structured future project fields.
- Refine Certifications into consistent credential cards with unavailable details clearly shown rather than invented.
- Turn Currently Exploring into an interactive roadmap where selecting a topic reveals its learning focus.
- Improve Contact with stronger information hierarchy, a polished form, and the existing email-draft behavior.
- Finish the footer with clearer VK branding and the Learn → Build → Innovate → Grow message.
- Add scroll-reveal motion with reduced-motion support and verify desktop and mobile layouts.

## Content safeguards
- Keep all education dates, skills, certification names, email, and location exactly as supplied.
- Do not invent projects, employers, achievements, links, issuers, completion dates, or proficiency levels.
- Keep LinkedIn, GitHub, portrait, certificates, and projects as polished unavailable states until real material is provided.
